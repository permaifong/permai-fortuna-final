From a pap smear to family planning or a check-up in pregnancy, you are seen in a private room, and the doctor explains what is happening and what comes next. If you would rather see a woman, ask for Dr. Valerianne.

Pap smear

What happens at a pap smear

A pap smear checks the cells of your cervix (the neck of the womb) for early changes that could turn into cervical cancer. Changes found early can be treated before cancer starts.

You lie on a couch in a private room. The doctor gently opens the vagina with a small instrument called a speculum and takes a sample from the cervix with a small, soft brush. It takes a few minutes. The sample goes to a lab, and the doctor goes through the result with you.

Ask about a pap smear
  • For women aged 20 to 65 who have ever had sex
  • After two normal yearly results, once every 3 years
  • Best done when you are not on your period
  • No sex, vaginal creams or douching for 2 days before
  • Ask for a female doctor if you prefer

Questions people ask

Who should have a pap smear?

Women aged 20 to 65 who have ever had sex, including women who feel well, because early changes in the cervix cause no symptoms.

How often do I need a pap smear?

If your first two yearly pap smears are normal, you then need one every 3 years. The doctor will tell you when your next one is due.

Does a pap smear hurt?

It can feel uncomfortable for a moment, but it should not hurt. Tell the doctor if it does and they will stop.

Can I see a female doctor?

Yes. Dr. Valerianne is in Monday to Friday, 7am to 12 noon, and Saturday, 7am to 11pm. Ask for her at the counter.

Can I have a pap smear during my period?

It is best done when you are not on your period, so the sample is clear. If your period starts, WhatsApp us and come another day.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in any time, day or night. To see our female doctor, come during her hours.

How do I find out the cost?

WhatsApp us or ask at the counter. The doctor's consultation is charged separately.
