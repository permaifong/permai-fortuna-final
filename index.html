Frequently asked questions.

Do you offer knee pain treatment and knee injection therapy?+
Yes. Our doctors assess and treat knee pain, and we offer intra-articular knee injection therapy for suitable patients, including those with knee osteoarthritis. Walk in for an assessment, no referral needed.
What is an intra-articular (knee) injection?+
It is an injection given directly into the knee joint to reduce pain and inflammation and improve movement, commonly used for osteoarthritis. Our doctor will examine your knee first and advise whether the injection is right for you.
Are you really open 24 hours?+
Yes. We are open 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including weekends and public holidays.
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?+
Walk-ins are welcome at any hour, day or night. If you have questions before coming, message us on WhatsApp at 012-939 3537.
Do you offer pre-employment health screening?+
Yes. We provide pre-employment medical examinations and health screening packages, with the report and forms completed for your employer.
Do you offer health screening or medical check-up packages?+
Yes. We offer medical check-up and health screening packages for individuals and pre-employment, including blood tests, lab screening, ECG heart checks and ultrasound scans. Walk in any time, no appointment needed.
Can I get a blood test at the clinic?+
Yes. We carry out blood tests and lab screening, either on their own or as part of a check-up. Walk in and our doctor will arrange the tests you need.
Do you have ECG and ultrasound services?+
Yes. We provide ECG heart checks and ultrasound scans at the clinic. Walk in for an assessment and the doctor will advise which tests are appropriate.
Do you offer antenatal check-ups?+
Yes. We provide routine antenatal follow-ups and pregnancy monitoring for expectant mothers on site, when the doctor finds them suitable.
Do you treat children and families?+
Yes. We see patients of all ages, from babies and children to grandparents, including childhood immunisations and everyday family health care.
Do you provide emergency treatment?+
Yes. We handle urgent conditions around the clock and provide treatment when the doctor finds it necessary. For life-threatening emergencies, please call 999 or head to the nearest hospital emergency department.
Where is the clinic located?+
We are on the ground floor of Fortuna Commercial Centre, Mile 2.5, Jalan Penampang, Kota Kinabalu, with parking right outside. See the map below for directions.
