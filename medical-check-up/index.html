How a check-up goes

Most of it is done in one visit.

Walk in or message us

Come in any time, day or night, or WhatsApp us to plan your visit. Bring your IC.

Fasting

For cholesterol and fasting blood sugar, do not eat for 10 to 12 hours before. Plain water is fine.

Tests in one visit

Blood is taken, and the ECG and any scan are done in the clinic, so you are not sent elsewhere.

Results explained

When your results are ready, the doctor goes through them with you and explains what to do next.

Questions people ask

How much is a medical check-up?

The Basic Health Screening is RM100 and the Full Health Screening is RM380. Single blood tests start from RM15, and an ECG or ultrasound is RM50. Prices do not include the doctor's consultation.

Do I need to fast before a check-up?

Only for cholesterol and fasting blood sugar tests. Do not eat for 10 to 12 hours before, but plain water is fine. Both packages include these tests, so come fasted.

How long until I get my results?

It depends on the tests you take. We will tell you when to expect them on the day, and the doctor goes through the results with you.

Can I come for a check-up at night?

Yes. The clinic is open 24 hours, so you can come before or after work.

Is the doctor consultation included?

No. Package and test prices cover the tests only. The doctor's consultation is charged separately.

Can I get just a blood test?

Yes. Single tests are listed above. Walk in and the doctor will arrange what you need.

Berapa harga pemeriksaan kesihatan?

Pakej Basic Health Screening RM100 dan Full Health Screening RM380. Harga tidak termasuk konsultasi doktor. Hantar WhatsApp jika ada soalan.
