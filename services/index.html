Everything we do, in one place

A general practitioner clinic open 24 hours on Jalan Penampang. Walk in for any of these, or WhatsApp us first if you have a question.

Doctor consultations

See a doctor any hour of the day or night. No appointment needed.

24-hour walk-in consultation

See a doctor any hour, no appointment needed, including public holidays. Fever, infections, injuries and anything else that worries you.

General consultation

Diagnosis and treatment for everyday illness in adults and children: colds, flu, fever, infections, stomach upsets.

House calls

The doctor visits you at home for a consultation, treatment or follow-up. Useful for elderly, bedridden or less mobile patients. Call us to arrange.

Urgent care

Urgent problems are seen around the clock.

Emergency treatment

Urgent conditions treated at any hour. For life-threatening emergencies, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

Nebuliser

Wound care and dressing

Questions people ask

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in any time, day or night. If you have a question first, WhatsApp us at 012-939 3537.

Can the doctor come to my home?

Yes. We do house calls for consultations, treatment and follow-ups, which helps elderly, bedridden or less mobile patients. Call us to arrange one.

Do you see children?

Yes. We see patients of all ages, from babies to grandparents.

Is there a doctor at night?

Yes. A doctor and a nurse are on duty every hour of every day.

What if my problem is an emergency?

We treat urgent conditions around the clock. For life-threatening emergencies, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
